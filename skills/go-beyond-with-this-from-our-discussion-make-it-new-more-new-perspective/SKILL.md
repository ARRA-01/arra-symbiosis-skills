# /go-beyond-with-this-from-our-discussion-make-it-new-more-new-perspective

We just had a discussion. Something was built, decided, or explored. Now GO BEYOND it.

## Steps

1. **Recall the thread** — What did we just discuss? What was built? What was the core insight?
2. **Find the unspoken** — What did we NOT say? What assumption went unchallenged? What question did we avoid?
3. **Push 3 layers deeper** — Each layer goes further than the last:

### Layer 1: The Obvious Next Step
What logically follows from what we just did? The step anyone would take.

### Layer 2: The Adjacent Possible
What becomes possible NOW that didn't exist before? What doors did we just open without realizing?

### Layer 3: The Paradigm Shift
If you take this idea to its extreme — what does the world look like? What breaks? What becomes unnecessary? What new problem emerges that's MORE interesting than the original one?

## Output format

```
🧵 Thread: <what we just discussed, in one line>

### Layer 1: Next Step
<the obvious continuation>

### Layer 2: Adjacent Possible
<what's now possible that wasn't before>
→ Concrete: <one specific thing to try>

### Layer 3: Paradigm Shift
<the extreme version — what breaks, what becomes unnecessary>
→ New question: <the more interesting problem that emerges>

### 🎯 One Bold Move
<if you could only do ONE thing right now to go beyond, what is it?>
```

## Rules

- Never repeat what was already said — only NEW perspectives
- Layer 3 should feel slightly uncomfortable — that's how you know you've gone far enough
- The "One Bold Move" must be actionable TODAY, not hypothetical
- Draw from the ENTIRE conversation, not just the last message
- Cross-reference with other repos, projects, or ideas if connections exist
- If the discussion was about code, think about what it means for the USER, not just the system
- If the discussion was about ideas, think about what it means for the CODE, not just the concept
